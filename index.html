Directive 01

Welcome, Guide

This is an Advanced (B2/C1) Sci-Fi Masterclass.

Welcome to the Multiverse. This class explores the concept of the Butterfly Effect, alternate realities, and the psychological weight of our past decisions.

Grammar Targets:

  • 🔀 Conditionals: 2nd, 3rd, and Mixed Conditionals to hypothesize about alternate paths.
  • 💭 Regret: Expressing deep regret using "Wish" and "If Only".

Warm-Up Phase

The Butterfly Effect

🦋

"Think of one small decision you made years ago. How would your life be different today if you had chosen the opposite?"

🗣️ Discussion Prompt:

"If I hadn't [ past action ], I probably wouldn't be [ current state ] today."

Warm-Up Phase

Infinite Possibilities

🌌

"Do you believe in the Multiverse Theory? The idea that every time you make a choice, a new universe is created where you made the other choice?"

Have the student argue for or against this concept before we dive into the sci-fi elements.

Psychological Vocabulary

The Mind's Lexicon

Click the glowing minds to reveal B2/C1 vocabulary for this sci-fi thriller.

🧠

Abduction

The action of taking someone away by force.

"Jason's abduction changed everything."

🗣️ Discussion Prompt:

What is the most famous story of an alien abduction you've heard?

👥

Doppelgänger

A biologically unrelated look-alike, or a double of a living person.

"He was kidnapped by his own doppelgänger."

🗣️ Discussion Prompt:

If you met an exact replica of yourself, would you trust them?

🛤️

Parallel

Occurring at the same time and in a similar way, but never intersecting.

"There are infinite parallel universes."

🗣️ Discussion Prompt:

Do you think a parallel version of you is doing exactly what you are doing right now?

🔄

To Swap

To exchange one thing for another.

"The kidnapper wanted to swap lives with him."

🗣️ Discussion Prompt:

If you could swap lives with a famous person for one day, who would it be?

Grammar Module

Navigating Realities

We use different Conditionals to navigate alternate lives and hypothetical pasts.

2nd Conditional (Unreal Present)

"If I were single, I would travel more."

3rd Conditional (Unreal Past)

"If he had built the box, he would have lost his family."

Mixed Conditional (Past affects Present)

"If I hadn't married her (past), I would be a CEO today (present)."

Grammar Module

Expressing Regret

To express deep regrets about the past, we use Wish or If Only + Past Perfect.

"I wish I had spent more time with them."

"If only I hadn't opened that door."
